1. Purpose

This policy sets out who may commit the funds of Sitara Welfare Trust and how each commitment is recorded. It exists so that staff can move quickly on routine purchases while ensuring that larger amounts receive independent review. The policy applies to every department, project, and field office.

2. Principles

Every purchase must serve an approved programme or operational need. Staff must obtain value for money, avoid conflicts of interest, and keep supporting documents for seven years. A request may not be divided into smaller requests to avoid an approval limit. If a purchase is urgent and the normal approver is unavailable, the next senior approver must act, and the reason for the substitution must be recorded.

3. Approval Limits

A department head may approve expenditure up to Rs. 250,000 for a single commitment. The Finance Director may approve expenditure up to Rs. 2,000,000. Any commitment above Rs. 2,000,000 requires the written approval of the Chief Executive Officer. Commitments above Rs. 10,000,000 also require a resolution of the Board. These limits apply to the total value of a contract, including taxes and expected variation, not merely to the first payment.

4. Documentation

Each approval must include a short description of the need, a quotation or tender summary, the budget line, and confirmation that funds are available. The finance team checks the approval before releasing payment. Missing or inconsistent documents are returned to the requesting department. The finance team keeps a register of approvals above Rs. 250,000 and reports it to the audit committee every quarter.

5. Review

The Finance Director reviews these limits each year and recommends changes to the Board. Any change takes effect only after written notice to all departments. This fictional sample exists to exercise the ingestion pipeline and is not legal or financial advice.
