1. Purpose

Sitara Welfare Trust ("the Trust") publishes this leave policy so that every member of staff understands how leave is requested, approved, recorded, and paid. The policy supports a healthy balance between the Trust's programme work and the wellbeing of the people who deliver it. Managers must apply the policy consistently and must not create informal arrangements that contradict it. The Trust reviews the policy every two years with staff representatives.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees of the Trust. Consultants, interns, and volunteers are covered only when their written agreement says so. The policy is issued by the Human Resources department. Questions about eligibility should be sent to the HR office before leave is booked, and any exception must be approved in writing by the Head of HR. Local office practices may add notice periods, but they may not reduce the entitlements described here.

3. Annual Leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to 20 working days of paid annual leave in each calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Leave accrues monthly, and up to five unused days may be carried into the following year with the manager's approval. Requests should normally be submitted at least 1.5 days before the planned absence, except in an emergency. The Trust encourages staff to take leave regularly. Staff may split leave into shorter periods, e.g. three separate weeks, provided cover is agreed with the line manager. Leave is recorded in the HR system on the first working day after the employee returns, and the record must match the approved request.

4. Sick Leave

Employees who are unwell should inform their manager as soon as possible on the first day of absence. Paid sick leave is available for up to 10 working days in a calendar year. A medical certificate is required after 2 consecutive sick days. The certificate must be issued by a registered practitioner and submitted to HR within three working days of the employee's return. Sick leave cannot be used in place of annual leave, and managers may not ask an employee to work while on certified sick leave. Repeated short absences may be discussed with the employee, but the discussion must remain confidential and focused on support.

5. Maternity and Paternity Leave

A pregnant employee is entitled to 90 calendar days of paid maternity leave, which may begin up to four weeks before the expected date of birth. A further 30 days of unpaid leave may be requested. Employees adopting a child are entitled to the same leave on the same terms. Paternity leave of 10 working days is available to a parent following the birth or adoption of a child and must be taken within six months. Employees should notify HR at least eight weeks before the intended start date where practicable.

6. Leave Encashment

Unused annual leave may be encashed once per year, subject to the following conditions:
(a) the employee has completed at least one year of continuous service;
(b) the request is approved by the line manager and the Head of HR; and
(c) the employee retains at least five days of annual leave after encashment.

Encashment is capped at Rs. 50,000 per calendar year and is paid with the following month's salary. Encashment is not available for sick leave, maternity leave, or paternity leave. On leaving the Trust, an employee is paid for accrued but unused annual leave at the salary rate in force on the final working day.
